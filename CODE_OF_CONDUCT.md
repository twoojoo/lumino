# Code of conduct

Lumino follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, and keep discussions about the work.
Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not tolerated in issues, pull requests, discussions or
any other project space.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer,
[@twoojoo](https://github.com/twoojoo). Reports are handled confidentially; the maintainer may
remove comments, close threads or ban participants who violate this code.
